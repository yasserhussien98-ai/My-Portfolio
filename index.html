The short version

How I ended up here

I studied German language and literature at Ain Shams University, expecting words and grammar — and ended up somewhere far more technical. Support work has a way of doing that to you: you start out solving one person's problem, and eventually you're the one who understands the whole system.

These days that means Exchange Online and Office 365 — enterprise mail flow, tickets that escalate at the worst possible moment, and customers who speak German and want a calm, clear answer, not just a fix.

"Experience becomes more valuable when it's shared" — which is the whole reason I write.

Outside of tickets, I'm still the same person who got a language degree out of curiosity: I like understanding the why behind a problem, not just closing it out.

What I'm good at

In practice

Day to day, that means Exchange Online administration and Office 365 support, a lot of ticket escalation, and enough Power Automate to stop doing the boring parts by hand. I move between German and English support without missing detail either way, and I've learned that customer relationship management is really just remembering that a ticket number is a person waiting on you.
